1. Your name, company and website.

## Why this licence

Option Hub took a lot of work to build and keeps getting better. Keeping it **free for individual traders and learners** is the point. Asking businesses that earn money from it to take a licence keeps the project sustainable, and stops it being repackaged and sold back to the same traders.
